Meet people who are NOT LIKE you.

notlike.us is a friendship app that introduces you to people who are different from you. Another faith, another culture, another generation, the other side of the aisle. That’s the point.

Why

The unknown is a boogeyman.

Most of us only know people like ourselves, so the people who aren’t become a headline or a stereotype. It’s hard to fear someone you’ve shared a meal with. notlike.us makes that first meal easy to set up, with someone you’d never otherwise meet.

Tell us about you

Answer a few quick questions: faith, background, politics, where you grew up and more. Skip anything you like.

See who’s different

We show you the people most unlike you first, with your differences right on the card.

Swipe to say hello

Swipe right to say hello, left to pass. Nobody is ever told about a pass, and it’s only a match when you both say hello.

Meet up

Chat in the app, then plan a first meetup around something you both enjoy, like a meal, a hike or a game night.

For everyone

Singles meet singles. Couples meet couples. Groups meet groups.

Join on your own, as a couple, or as a group like a church, mosque, temple, club or team. You’re only matched with others who joined the same way, so a couple from Montana meets a couple from Atlanta, and a congregation meets a congregation.

Couples

Episcopalians from Atlanta and ranchers from Montana, trading Sunday dinners.

Groups

A mosque women’s circle and a gospel church, sharing an iftar and a potluck.

Safe by design

Built for friendship, with safety first.

  • Adults onlyEveryone confirms they’re 18 or older before joining.
  • Real photos, checkedEvery profile photo is reviewed before anyone else can see it.
  • You choose what to shareEvery question can be skipped. Sensitive answers stay private unless you choose to show them.
  • Private swipesNobody sees who you passed on, and nobody can message you unless you both said hello.
  • Block and reportUnmatch, block or report anyone in two taps, and delete your account any time.
